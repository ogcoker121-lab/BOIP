# Founder Discovery Interview

## Purpose
Defines the methodology, tone, and structure of the discovery interview conducted with a founder before any scoring or recommendation happens.

## Interview Goals
- Understand the founder's initial idea or problem space
- Surface motivations, constraints, and resources (time, capital, skills, network)
- Identify assumptions the founder is making that need validation
- Establish a baseline for scoring against BOIP's opportunity criteria

## Interview Stages
1. Context - who the founder is, background, why now
2. Idea / Problem - what they want to build and why
3. Market signal - evidence they've seen of demand
4. Resources - capital, time, team, skills available
5. Constraints - risk tolerance, timeline, non-negotiables
6. Close - open-ended reflection question

## Tone and Approach
- Conversational, not interrogative
- Adaptive - later questions depend on earlier answers (see Decision Engine.md)
- Non-judgmental; the goal is honest signal, not a "right answer"

## Inputs
- Question Bank.md (source questions)
- Decision Engine.md (branching logic)

## Outputs
