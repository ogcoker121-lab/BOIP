# Question Bank

## Purpose
Categorized repository of interview questions used by the Founder Discovery Interview.

## Categories

### 1. Founder Context
- What is your professional background?
- Have you started a business before? What happened?
- Why this idea, and why now?

### 2. Problem & Idea
- What problem are you trying to solve?
- Who experiences this problem most acutely?
- How do people solve this problem today?

### 3. Market Signal
- What evidence do you have that people want this?
- Have you talked to potential customers? What did they say?
- Who are your closest competitors?

### 4. Resources
- How much capital can you commit in the first 12 months?
- How much time per week can you dedicate?
- What skills or team gaps do you have?

### 5. Risk & Constraints
- What would make you walk away from this idea?
- What's your timeline to first revenue?
- What's your risk tolerance (conservative / moderate / aggressive)?

### 6. Closing
- What's the one thing you're most worried about?
- What does success look like in year one?

## Notes
- Each question should map to one or more scoring dimensions in Scoring Engine.md
- New questions should be added here, not inline in the interview logic
